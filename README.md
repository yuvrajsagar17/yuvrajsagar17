## 👋 Hey, I'm Yuvraj Sagar  

I’m a **Machine Learning Engineer** focused on building *efficient, scalable, and intelligent AI systems* powered by *LLMs* and their core frameworks.   

With a strong foundation in both software engineering and machine learning frameworks, I specialize in:  
- **Training, fine-tuning, merging, and evaluating** LLMs for specialized domains  
- **Developing optimized CUDA kernels** for accelerating model training and inference  
- **Designing RAG pipelines** and **agentic workflows** that enhance contextual reasoning and autonomy  

Inspired by [llm.c](https://github.com/karpathy/llm.c) by *Andrej Karpathy*, I’ve implemented the **LLaMA architecture** in **CUDA C/C++**, experimenting with custom kernels and GPU throughput optimization.  

Currently, I’m exploring multimodal and agentic AI systems, integrating speech, text, vision, and reasoning capabilities to build next-generation conversational and interactive intelligence

### ⚙️ Engineering & Research Focus  

- **LLMs & Transformers:** Pretraining, fine-tuning, model merging, evaluation, and quantization (GGUF, GPTQ).  
- **RAG & Agentic AI Systems:** Building RAG and autonomous reasoning pipelines.  
- **CUDA & Systems Optimization:** Writing custom GPU kernels and optimizing model throughput for large-scale training and inference.  
- **Speech & ASR Models:** Working with opensource ASR Models such as *Whisper*, *HuBERT* etc., for real-time evaluation, conversational AI, and voice-based feedback systems.  
- **AI Applications:** End-to-end intelligent systems using LLMs, ASR models, Agentic Workflows, and Multimodal Architectures including VLMs, SLMs, and LMMs to build scalable, context-aware, and interactive AI products.  

### 🧠 Recent Projects  

- **Presentily**  
  AI-powered platform for improving speaking & presentation skills using *CrisperWhisper*, and opensource *HF Transformers* models. Features real-time AI feedback on fluency, confidence, vocabulary, and grammar, with *speech analytics* and *progress tracking*.  

- **[axolotl-finetune](https://github.com/yuvrajsagar17/axolotl-finetune)**  
  Single and Multi-GPU **SFT** of **LLaMA-3.1-8B** with [Axolotl](https://github.com/axolotl-ai-cloud/axolotl/) using different finetuning techniques such as LoRA, QLoRA, as well as Spectrum (targetted finetuning). Benchmarking LLM performance on Nous evaluation benchmarks, and qunatizing models into **GGUFs** via [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp).  
  [Check my models on Hugging Face →](https://huggingface.co/yuvraj17)  

- **[llama.c](https://github.com/yuvrajsagar17/llama.c)**  
  Reimplementation of **LLaMA3 architecture** in **CUDA C/C++**, with custom kernels for matrix multiplications and attention operations, inspired by Karpathy’s minimalist llm.c.  

- **NUST Bank Assistant (RAG System)**  
  Intelligent banking chatbot using *LLMs + RAG* with *FAISS*, *Qdrant*, *Cohere Rerank*, *SFT*, and *Guardrails*. Frontend developed using *Streamlit*
